MIDI

Off

In: ch 1 → notes engine, ch 2 → chords engine, ch 3 → bass, ch 10 → drums (GM drum notes), pitch bend, mod wheel (CC1) → vibrato, CC 70–73 → the four knobs. Out: notes on ch 1, chords on ch 2, bass on ch 3, drums on ch 10.

How to play

Chords (left hand): QWER / SDF — the 7 chords of the key.   Notes (right hand): UIOP[] / JKL;' — 11 steps of the scale, starting two notes below the key's root (the root is on O, outlined in pink).
Octave: Z/X for chords, ,/. for notes.   Key: ↑/↓ (or -/=, or the KEY UP / KEY DOWN buttons). Changing the key transposes a recorded loop with it. Scale / progression: ←/→, or the PROGRESSION button.   Looper: Space record on/off, Enter play/stop, 1 rec button (tap 3× while armed to set the tempo), 2 multiply, Backspace undo (hold it to clear), T tap (re-grid a free loop on its downbeats), Delete metronome on/off.
Loop timeline: each overdub is a track row. Click a row's switch to turn it on/off; click its length (right-click to go back) to make it shorter or longer. A track shorter than the loop repeats to fill it, and Multiply repeats every track instead of copying notes. Click a note to select it, then drag it or press ←/→ to move it a beat (Shift = one quantize step); Delete or Backspace removes it, Esc deselects. While a note is selected those keys act on the note; otherwise they do their usual jobs. Set New track to record a short track that repeats.
Arpeggiator: click the ARP switch at the top left or press ` to cycle Off → Up → Down → Random. Hold a chord and it plays the chord's notes one at a time. Set rate, gate and octaves in the Chords engine card.
Drums: press \ or use the NOTES / DRUMS / BASS switch at the top right to turn the right-hand pads into an 11-piece drum kit. The same keys play it.
Bass: the third right-hand mode (\ cycles Notes → Drums → Bass). The pads play a pitched, held 808-style kick: the hit plays once, then the tone sustains while you hold the key. It is mono, so a new note cuts the last one; turn up Glide to slide between notes. ,/. change its octave. Set Pads to Follow chord in the Bass card and the pads play the whole scale of whatever chord is sounding, starting from its root on O (the chord's own notes are outlined). Hold a pad and the bass follows every chord change you play, or the loop's chords when you're not holding one.
Knobs: drag up/down or use the mouse wheel. Double-click resets. Choose what each knob controls in the engine cards.
